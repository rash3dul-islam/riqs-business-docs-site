    AI features¶

    In one sentence: RIQS uses artificial intelligence (AI) to translate content into each user's own language and to answer questions about documents in RiQS-Pedia. Menu: No own menu. AI appears as buttons inside other modules (Translate, Ask RiQS AI). · Status: Mixed: see the table below

    What it is¶

    Many RIQS customers have teams who speak different languages. A task written in German may be read by a colleague whose first language is Portuguese. AI translation lets that colleague see the text in their own language with one click.

    The second feature is an AI assistant called RiQS AI. You ask it a question in normal words, for example "What is our procedure for cleaning the autoclave?". It searches the documents in RiQS-Pedia and answers. It also shows the documents its answer comes from.

    AI use costs money for the provider, so it is measured in tokens (small pieces of text). Each organisation buys a token budget as part of its subscription.

    Status at a glance¶

    Feature Where Status
    AI translation (Translate button) Monitoring, To-Dos, Training, Process Guide, Forms (Configurator) details Live
    RiQS AI chat for documents RiQS-Pedia (Ask AI button in the toolbar) In progress: built in the new web app, rollout to be confirmed
    Ask RiQS AI about a device Equipment details (Ask RiQS AI button) Planned: the button is shown but does not do anything yet
    Token budget and usage Organisation and unit subscription pages Live

    Key terms¶

    Term Meaning
    AI translation Automatic translation of text written by people (tasks, answers, remarks) into the reader's language.
    Native language The language set as "native language" in your user profile. Translation always goes into this language.
    RiQS AI The AI assistant that answers questions about RiQS-Pedia documents.
    Sources The documents RiQS AI used for its answer. They are listed under the answer.
    Token A unit used to measure AI use. Longer texts use more tokens.
    Language tokens The token budget for AI translation.
    Manual tokens The token budget shown on the subscription page for the AI assistant (see Open questions).

    Who uses it¶

    Role What they typically do here
    RiQS Administrator Sets token budgets for offline customers; checks usage.
    Group Administrator Uses translation and RiQS AI. Token rights: see Open questions.
    Administrator Buys tokens and shares them between units; checks usage.
    Power User Buys extra tokens for the unit; checks the unit's usage.
    Management Uses translation and RiQS AI.
    Professional Uses translation and RiQS AI.

    How it works¶

    AI translation (Live)¶

    Open item → Translate → text shown in your language → Translate again → original text

    1. You open a task, To-Do, training, process guide or form that someone wrote in another language.
    2. You click Translate.
    3. RIQS checks that:
    4. you have a native language in your profile
    5. the language is supported
    6. your unit has tokens left
    7. the subscription is active
    8. The texts on the page are shown in your language. The button is highlighted while the translation is shown.
    9. Click Translate again to go back to the original text.

    RiQS AI chat (In progress)¶

    Open RiQS-Pedia → Ask AI → choose documents (optional) → ask → answer with sources

    1. In RiQS-Pedia, click the AI button in the toolbar. The RiQS AI panel opens: "Hello! I am RiQS AI".
    2. Optional: click Select Document to limit the question to certain documents.
    3. Type your question and send it. The answer appears word by word.
    4. Under the answer, Sources shows which documents were used.
    5. You can ask follow-up questions in the same chat. When you close the panel, the chat starts fresh next time.

    How to …¶

    How to translate content into your language¶

    Who can do this: all roles who can open the item 1. Make sure your native language is set in your user profile. 2. Open the task, To-Do, training, process guide or form. 3. Click Translate.

    Result: The text is shown in your native language. Click Translate again to see the original.

    How to ask RiQS AI a question (In progress)¶

    Who can do this: users who can open RiQS-Pedia 1. Open RiQS-Pedia and click the AI button in the toolbar. 2. Optional: choose documents with Select Document. 3. Type your question in Type here and send it.

    Result: RiQS AI answers and lists its Sources.

    How to check or buy tokens¶

    Who can do this: Administrator (organisation); Power User (own unit); RiQS Administrator (organisations licensed offline) 1. Open the organisation or unit details and go to the subscription section. 2. Check Language Tokens and Manual Tokens: used and remaining. 3. Use the subscription update to buy more tokens.

    Result: The new balance is available right away. The purchase is added to the next bill. See the subscription page for prices and rules.

    Rules to know¶

    • Translation goes into your native language only. If your profile has no native language, you see "User native language not found".
    • Supported languages:
    • English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese
    • Russian, Ukrainian, Serbian, Croatian, Albanian, Bulgarian
    • Turkish, Czech, Polish, Romanian, Hungarian
    • Tamil, Tagalog
    • Translations are not saved into the item. The original text stays as written. A translation you already made on the page is reused without using new tokens.
    • No tokens, no translation. When the token budget is used up you see "Token limit exceeded". When the subscription has expired, translation stops too.
    • Budgets exist at organisation and unit level. The organisation buys tokens and can share them out to units.
    • Low-balance alerts. The feature list says admins and Power Users get an alert when tokens run low.
    • RiQS AI and confidentiality. The rule is that RiQS AI only uses documents you are allowed to see (see Open questions).
    • RiQS AI does not keep chat history after you close the panel. Every question and answer is still logged for usage counting.

    Connected modules¶

    • Monitoring, Open Items, Training, Process Guide and Configurator: show the Translate button.
    • RiQS-Pedia: the documents RiQS AI searches.
    • Equipment: will get its own Ask RiQS AI button (Planned).
    • Users: the native language in the user profile decides the translation language.
    • Subscription & billing: token budgets and purchases.
    • Reports and downloads: translated remarks appear in downloaded reports (according to the feature list).

    Open questions¶

    • Status of AI translation. The product list marks AI translation as planned, but the Translate button is live in five modules. Please confirm it is released for customers.
    • Status of RiQS AI. RiQS AI is built in RiQS-Pedia. Is it released, and for which customers?
    • What "Manual tokens" are. The subscription page shows "Manual Tokens". RiQS AI chat usage appears to be counted there. Is that the customer-facing name for AI assistant tokens?
    • RiQS AI and rights. The feature list says RiQS AI must only use documents the user may see. Please confirm this is enforced, also when no document is selected.
    • Translation scope. The feature list also mentions translating remarks in Users, Equipment and Risk Management. The current app shows Translate only in Monitoring, To-Dos, Training, Process Guide and Forms. Are the others still planned?
    • Equipment AI. What will Ask RiQS AI do on a device page? (For example: answer questions from the device manual.) When is it planned?
    • Group Administrator and tokens. The subscription feature list names only the Administrator (organisation) and the Power User (unit). What may a Group Administrator see and buy?
    • Who may use RiQS AI. Can every role open it, and does the RiQS Administrator use the customer's tokens when testing?
